Fungal Pathogens
Ascomycetes & Basidiomycetes

Spore germ tubes penetrate through leaf cuticles and stomata. Produce concentric "bullseye" rings, powdery white mycelium, and anthracnose lesions.

Target rings (Alternaria)
Powdery coating (Podosphaera)
Black rot mummies (Botryosphaeria)
💊 Treated with Chlorothalonil, Mancozeb, Difenoconazole

Bacterial Infections
Xanthomonas & Ralstonia

Bacteria enter through natural hydathodes or pruning wounds. Cause water-soaked dark lesions, yellow chlorotic halos, and shot-hole perforations.

Water-soaked dark lesions
Yellow chlorotic halo ring
Shot-hole leaf drop
💊 Treated with Copper Hydroxide & Oxytetracycline
🧬
Viral Pathogens
Begomovirus & Tobamovirus

Sub-microscopic RNA/DNA viruses spread via insect vectors (whiteflies/aphids) or tools. Cause leaf distortion, mosaic mottling, and severe stunting.

Upward leaf curling (TYLCV)
Mottled mosaic green pattern
Shoestring leaf thinning
🛡️ Vector control via Imidacloprid & Sticky traps
🕷️
Mites & Pest Damage
Tetranychus urticae

Microscopic arachnid mites puncture individual plant cells to suck out chlorophyll. Results in chlorotic stippling and delicate silken protective webbing.

Chlorotic pinpoint stippling
Silken underside webbing
Bronzed desiccated leaves
💊 Treated with Abamectin, Spiromesifen & Neem Oil

🧠 Deep Learning Architecture

Two-Stage EfficientNet-B0 Transfer Learning

How compound scaling and multi-stage fine-tuning maximize classification accuracy while minimizing training time and compute.

Slide 06 / 10
❄️ Stage 1: Feature Extraction Epochs 1–8 (lr = 1e-3)

Frozen Backbone + Custom Head

EfficientNet-B0 pretrained ImageNet weights (~5.3M parameters) are completely frozen. The model quickly learns domain-specific features in the newly attached Dense softmax layer without destroying pre-learned representations.

EfficientNetB0 Backbone (237 Layers) ❄️ Frozen (0 trainable)
GlobalAveragePooling2D + Dropout(0.2) ⚡ Trainable
Dense(10, activation='softmax') ⚡ 12,810 Trainable Params
🎯 Stage 1 Val Accuracy: ~94.2% (Rapid Convergence)
🔥 Stage 2: Fine-Tuning Epochs 9–20 (lr = 1e-5)

Unfreeze Top 20 Backbone Layers

We selectively unfreeze the top 20 convolutional blocks (including depthwise separable convolutions and squeeze-and-excitation blocks) with a very low learning rate (1e-5) to adapt high-level visual features specifically to plant lesions.

Lower 217 EfficientNet Layers ❄️ Kept Frozen
Top 20 MBConv Blocks 🔥 Fine-Tuning (~1.2M Params)
Custom Classification Head ⚡ Fine-Tuning
🚀 Stage 2 Val Accuracy: ~98.7% (High Precision)

🔍 Hard Classification Pairs Analysis

Septoria Leaf Spot vs. Bacterial Spot:

Both present small dark circular spots. Fine-tuned EfficientNet identifies pycnidia black fruiting dots in Septoria centers vs. water-soaked margins in Bacterial Spot.

Early Blight vs. Late Blight:

Distinguished by concentric target rings in Early Blight (*Alternaria*) vs. irregular fuzzy water-soaked lesions in Late Blight (*Phytophthora*).

Healthy Control vs. Mild Chlorosis:

Clean 99.8% precision with virtually zero false-positive alerts on non-infected crops.
