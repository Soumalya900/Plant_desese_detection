Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026. Document ID: LC-PRIV-2026-V2

Who we are

LeafCheck is operated by PlantCare Pathology Suite. Contact: privacy@plantcare.ai.

What we collect

  • The leaf photos you upload for real-time model inference.
  • Basic technical telemetry such as browser type and approximate region, used to maintain service performance.

We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to run a leaf diagnosis check.

How we use it

Your photo is processed transiently in server memory to produce the disease report shown to you. We do not sell your photos or use them for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Uploaded leaf images processed during live diagnostic queries are cleared from active server memory immediately after inference.

Who processes your data

Photos are analyzed by our secure MobileNet deep learning classifier endpoints. Data is processed solely to deliver your diagnostic report.

Your rights

You can request to view, correct, or purge data held about your session under applicable digital privacy legislation.

Children

LeafCheck is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated revision date will appear at the top of this document.
