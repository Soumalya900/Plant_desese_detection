Most likely diagnosis

Early blight

Alternaria solani

Match strength: High (92% confidence)

Pathology & Symptoms

Brown spots with concentric target rings starting on foliage. Leaves around lesions show chlorotic yellowing.

What to do now

  1. Remove and dispose of severely affected leaves immediately.
  2. Water at the base of the plant to avoid keeping leaves wet.
  3. Apply recommended copper or Mancozeb fungicide spray according to pack instructions.
  4. Re-inspect the crop after 7 days for new symptoms.

Diagnostic Parameters

  • Disease Type: Fungal
  • Affected Area: Leaf Foliage
  • Severity Level: Moderate
  • Action Level: Treatment Advised

Ask a local agricultural extension expert if

Spots reach new growth within 7 days, or more than a quarter of the plants display severe lesions.
